# Clean Your Shoes

Source: [[resources/shoe-care/2026-09-11 - Clean Sneakers by Material|Clean Sneakers by Material]]

## Checklist

- [ ] Remove laces and insoles
- [ ] Brush off loose dirt/dust first (dry)
- [ ] Scrub outsole with soapy water + old toothbrush
- [ ] Spot test any cleaner on a hidden area first
- [ ] Clean the upper based on material — soap + water for canvas/mesh/leather; dry brush + suede eraser only (no water) for suede/nubuck
- [ ] Wipe off soap residue with a damp cloth
- [ ] Hand-wash laces separately; wipe insoles without soaking
- [ ] Stuff with paper and air dry away from sun/heat
- [ ] Reapply protector spray once fully dry

## Notes

- Frequency: about once a month for a pair worn a few times a week.
- Never wet suede/nubuck panels — dry brush and eraser only.
- New Balance 740 and Saucony ProGrid: soap + brush on mesh, but treat suede overlay panels as suede.
- Adidas Adizero EVO SL: skip scrubbing, gentle damp wipe only — the foam upper is delicate.
- Air Force 1 tolerates a full wet-clean; watch the white midsole for sun yellowing.
