# Personal Development

## Purpose

Maintain deliberate self-improvement: building good habits, breaking bad ones, and sustaining motivation over time.

## Standard to Maintain

- Run new habits through a deliberate design process instead of relying on willpower.
- Capture lessons from habits that stick and habits that fail.
- Keep reusable behavior-change knowledge organized in [[resources/behavior-change/behavior-change|Behavior Change resources]].

## Review Rhythm

Review monthly, or during a weekly review when a habit is plateauing or slipping.
