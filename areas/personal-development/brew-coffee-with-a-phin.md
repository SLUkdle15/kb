# Brew Coffee with a Phin

## Checklist

- [ ] Add grounds, then shake or tap gently to level the bed
- [ ] Seat the filter disc on top and press down until it just touches the coffee — no gap, no crushing
- [ ] Bloom: pour just enough water (90-96°C) to saturate the grounds, roughly covering the disc
- [ ] Wait 20-30 seconds — grounds puff slightly, a few drips may start
- [ ] Fill the chamber near the top with the rest of the water, poured slowly in a light circular motion or steady in the centre
- [ ] Put the lid on to hold heat and steady the drip
- [ ] Let it drip 4-5 minutes for a full one-cup phin

## Notes

- Phin: the Vietnamese metal drip filter (cà phê phin).
- Water off the boil, not straight from it — boiling water scorches the grounds.
- Never blast the second pour in fast or off to one side; it disturbs the bed and channels.
- Under 3 minutes means under-extracted: grind finer, or check the disc is seated properly.
- Past 6-7 minutes means over-extracted: grind coarser.
- A too-fast drip is the common failure — chase disc seating first, then grind size.
