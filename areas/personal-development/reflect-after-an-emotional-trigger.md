# Reflect After an Emotional Trigger

Source: Kristin Neff, *Self-Compassion*. Study material: [[resources/emotional-regulation/emotional-regulation|Emotional Regulation]]

Use once the heat drops, or in the evening.

## Checklist

- [ ] Pick what I felt bad about or judged myself for today.
- [ ] Write: what happened, what I felt, what I needed, what story my mind made.
- [ ] Reframe it — what would I say to a close friend? If "I am X" showed up, restate it as a conditional: [[resources/emotional-regulation/2026-08-19 - Common Humanity Loosens Fixed Self-Labels|Common Humanity Loosens Fixed Self-Labels]].
- [ ] Decide one wise action, including whether I need to speak up or set a limit, and route it into `next/next-actions` if it outlives tonight.

## Notes

- Judgement → understanding. The writing is where that shift happens.
- I don't need shame to grow — improvement comes through kindness, honesty, responsibility.
- Fierce compassion is protection, not resentment.
- Practicing the critic → boundary → compassion dialogue over weeks: [[resources/emotional-regulation/2026-08-18 - Self-Kindness in Practice|Self-Kindness in Practice]].
