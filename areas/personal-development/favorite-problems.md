1. How can I turn strong technical work into influence and real business value?
2. How can I become the kind of person who naturally creates leadership, ownership, and momentum in every group I join?
3. How can I become financially competent and build a strong personal system for money, tax, law, property, renting, buying, and selling?
4. How can I build a learning and intelligence system that makes progress automatic — spotting important ideas early and turning them into insight, decisions, and action?
5. How can I become an unstoppable competitor in the sports I play — badminton, soccer, golf, and whatever I take up next — with the strength, mobility, and endurance to keep performing for decades?
