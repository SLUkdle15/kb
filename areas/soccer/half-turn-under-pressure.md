# Half-Turn Under Pressure

Source: [[resources/soccer/2026-08-04 - Soccer Playing Cues|Soccer Playing Cues]]

Use when receiving the ball with a defender pressing from in front, in view.

## Checklist

- [ ] Before the ball arrives, check your shoulders — locate the pressure and the space.
- [ ] Confirm the press is known and in front, not an unseen angle. If unseen, this protocol doesn't apply — the half turn is for known pressure, not blind pressure.
- [ ] Plan the half turn now, so the first touch turns you — don't take a square touch that needs a second touch to reorient.
- [ ] Look forward first: is the vertical/penetrating pass on?
- [ ] If forward isn't on, switch or progress diagonally.
- [ ] Only recycle/reset if nothing forward is available.
- [ ] After the pass, move to support the receiver — the pass isn't finished until you've given him an option.
- [ ] Pick the support position by asking: if I were him, where would I want the passer to be? Then go there.

## Notes

- The half turn is a tool to turn away from known pressure, not to turn through unknown pressure — don't reach for it as a general answer to being marked.
- Pairs with the "do not turn into pressure" cue in the source note: this protocol is the concrete version of that principle for the in-front-press case specifically.
