**How to fill it in:**

- **Pass ratio** — one gut-feel number after the game, no counting needed.
- **Key passes** — tally in your head when it happens (rare enough to remember).
- **Ball recoveries** — tally in your head or count on your fingers during breaks.
- **Effort felt** — one word, right after the game.
- **Note** — one line, anything that stood out.
