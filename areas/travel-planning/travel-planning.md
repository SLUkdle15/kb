# Travel Planning

## Purpose

Maintain travel planning knowledge, preferences, logistics, documents, and future trip ideas.

## Standard to Maintain

- Keep useful travel references, packing notes, and logistics findable.
- Convert specific trips into projects.
- Keep someday destinations in `next/maybe` until they become real plans.

## Review Rhythm

Review monthly, and before planning a trip.
