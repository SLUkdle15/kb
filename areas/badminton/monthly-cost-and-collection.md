# Monthly Cost & Collection

Area: [[areas/badminton/badminton|Badminton]]

Cost basis for the [[next/calendar/2026-09-02 - Weekly Saturday Badminton|Weekly Saturday Badminton]] session, 4 sessions a month.

## Cost

| Item | Per session | Per month (4 sessions) | Per person / session |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Court | 210,000 | 840,000 | 35,000 (6p) / 30,000 (7p) |
| Shuttles (6 pcs) | 165,000 | 660,000 | 27,500 (6p) / 23,571 (7p) |
| **Total out** | **375,000** | **1,500,000** | **62,500 (6p) / 53,571 (7p)** |
| Collected @ 6 people | 378,000 | 1,512,000 | 63,000 |
| Collected @ 7 people | 378,000 | 1,512,000 | 54,000 |

Monthly shuttles: 6 × 4 = 24 pcs = 2 boxes @ 330,000.

Collected amounts are rounded up to a clean per-head number rather than the exact split, leaving a small surplus (~3,000/session, ~12,000/month) as buffer.
