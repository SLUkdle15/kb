# Romantic Relationship

## Purpose

Maintain the romantic relationship, communication habits, shared commitments, and recurring care.

## Standard to Maintain

- Keep the relationship visible and cared for.
- Track recurring commitments, important dates, and open conversations.
- Convert specific relationship actions into projects or next actions.

## Review Rhythm

Review monthly, or weekly when the relationship needs active attention.
