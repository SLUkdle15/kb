# Gift Ideas for Her

Area: [[areas/romantic-relationship/romantic-relationship|Romantic Relationship]]

A standing list, so a birthday or an anniversary does not start from a blank page. Entries marked **known** come from something already recorded about her; the rest are candidates to test.

## Everyday Things She'd Use

- **Thermal bottle.** **Known-adjacent** — she has low blood pressure, so warm water and ginger tea through the day are practical, not decorative. The most useful thing on this list.
- Warm socks, a small blanket, a scarf. Same reason: low blood pressure makes cold hands and cold feet a real daily thing rather than a preference.
- A good umbrella. Hanoi rain, and the weekday-dinner protocol already has a raincoat line in it for a reason.

## Beauty and Skincare

- **Makeup.** Needs her actual brands and shades before buying — this is the category where guessing wrong is most visible. Ask, or go with her once and note what she picks.
- **Sheet masks.** Low risk, easy to give in a set, easy to repeat.
- Lip balm or hand cream. The small ones that get used up and can be given again without ceremony.

## Things She Already Likes

- **Fruit.** **Known** — grapes, papaya, guava, oranges, tangerines, watermelon. A good box of any of these is not a lesser gift than an object.
- **The green of sunlight through leaves.** **Known** — a specific colour preference she named. Usable for anything that has a colour: a bag, a case, a scarf, a print, a plant.
- **Candy.** **Known** — it is on [[areas/romantic-relationship/2026-07-15 - Promises to Her|Promises to Her]].

## Experiences, Not Objects

Every one of the eight items on [[areas/romantic-relationship/2026-07-15 - Promises to Her|Promises to Her]] is something to do together, not something to own. That is the strongest signal in the vault about what she actually wants.

- A concert — **promised**
- A workshop — **promised**
- A coloring book to do together — **promised**, and it is both an object and an experience
- An out-of-city trip — **promised**
- LUPO, and The Scientist — **promised**

Giving one of these as a gift closes a promise at the same time. That is worth more than anything bought cold.

## Rule

After giving something, write down what she actually thought. A list that never records reactions stays a list of guesses.
