# Company Soccer

## Purpose

Keep the FPT company soccer group at my center (around 15 people) playing regularly and wanting to show up, now that the quarterly prize tournament has been dropped.

## Standard to Maintain

- The group has a field on Tuesday afternoon from 17:30. I go some weeks; other weeks I play the evening match with the Korean group instead ([[next/calendar/Weekly Tuesday Soccer|Weekly Tuesday Soccer]]).
- Turn any idea below that gets picked into a next action or a project.

## Review Rhythm

Review monthly, or after each outside match.

## Ideas to Boost the Group

- **Outside teams, about once a quarter.** Two opponents are interested: Minh's friends have asked to play, and CSOC is the neighbor. Alternate them so each one comes around about once a quarter. A match against another FPT team was played recently, so don't make this monthly.
- **Season table.** Track standings, goals and attendance in the shared player sheet, and post it after each match day.
- **End-of-quarter prize instead of a cash pot.** One item for the person who came most often or the top scorer: a trophy, jerseys, or a Garmin (see [[Untitled]]).
- **Small stakes.** The last-place team pays for the field or the first round of beer, so match days pay for themselves.
- **Different formats.** Mixed teams (shuffle SP1, SP2 and RD), a penalty shootout, 5-a-side futsal, old vs. young.
- **Social glue.** Beer after the match, or watch a big game together.
- **A small regular budget.** Ask the union (công đoàn) or the center for a small monthly amount for the field, instead of one big sponsorship.
