# Run a Beginner Range Session

Use for a single range session while stance, grip, and swing are still being built — the practice side of [[projects/self-taught-golf/self-taught-golf|Self-Taught Golf]].

About 60 balls and an hour. *Lead* is the side facing the target (left for a right-handed player); *trail* is the other side.

## Checklist

### Warm up — 10 balls, wedge

- [ ] Half swings only. No target, no scoring. Just get loose and feel the club hit the ball.

### Setup — repeat before every ball, all session

- [ ] Grip: the club runs across the base of the lead-hand fingers, not through the palm. Close the hand so about two knuckles show.
- [ ] Trail hand covers the lead thumb; the V between trail thumb and forefinger points at the trail shoulder.
- [ ] Grip pressure light. If a death grip is 10, hold at about 4 — tension kills the turn.
- [ ] Aim the clubface at the target *first*, then build the stance. Feet, hips, and shoulders run parallel to the target line, not pointed at the target.
- [ ] Posture: bend from the hips with the back fairly straight, knees soft, arms hanging under the shoulders, weight in the middle of the feet.
- [ ] Ball position: middle of the stance for a wedge or short iron; slightly forward, with a wider stance, as clubs get longer.

### Half swings — 20 balls, 7-iron or pitching wedge

- [ ] Lead arm to roughly 9 o'clock going back, and the same length coming through. Distance is not the point.
- [ ] Turn the chest back, turn it through. Do not lift the club with the arms.
- [ ] Finish with the weight on the lead foot and the belt buckle facing the target. Hold the finish two seconds before looking up.
- [ ] Check the turf: the divot or brushed grass should start at the ball or just past it, never before it.

### Full swings — 10 balls, same club

- [ ] Same setup, same turn, just longer. The moment contact falls apart, go back to half swings.

### Contact tally — 20 balls, one club

- [ ] Count how many of the 20 made clean contact.
- [ ] Note where on the face the strike tends to land: toe, heel, thin, or fat.
- [ ] Note which way the ball tends to go.
- [ ] Write the three numbers down the same day. An unrecorded tally is not a measurement.

## Notes

- One swing thought per session. Two swing thoughts is the same as none — pick the thing that was worst last time and leave everything else alone.
- Clean contact comes before distance and before direction. A well-struck short shot is progress; a long one off the toe is not.
- The tally is the actual output of the session. Balls hit without a number attached don't tell you whether anything changed.
