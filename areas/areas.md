# Areas

## What Belongs Here

Areas are ongoing responsibilities you maintain over time. They do not have a final completion date.

Examples:

- Health and fitness
- Career development
- Finance
- Home operations
- Relationships

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Short-term outcomes with deadlines
- General reference topics without personal responsibility
- Completed projects
- Temporary raw captures

## Review Frequency

Review monthly, or during a weekly review when an area is under active pressure.

Ask:

- [ ] Is this area healthy?
- [ ] What standard am I maintaining?
- [ ] Which projects should be created from this area?
- [ ] Which notes are outdated or should move to Resources or Archives?
