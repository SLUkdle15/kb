# Hồ Tây Walking

## Purpose

Build a habit of walking loops and routes around Hồ Tây, and keep an honest count of how many times it's been done. The goal is one full loop's worth of distance — Hồ Tây's shoreline is ≈17.35 km (OpenStreetMap) — accumulated across walks, not necessarily in one go.

## Standard to Maintain

- After each walk, send the start/end (and any waypoint) coordinates or Google Maps links so it gets added to the tracker.
- Keep the walk count and mapped routes in the tracker current — don't let walks pile up unrecorded.

## Review Rhythm

Review whenever a new walk is logged, or monthly otherwise.

## Walk Tracker

- [Vòng Hồ Tây](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/3cc20ef9-e796-4681-ae54-7f4bb3f5012f) — map artifact with one colour per walk and a running count. 4 walks logged as of 2026-09-13: ≈4.73 km of ≈17.35 km (one loop), ≈12.61 km left, roughly 11 more walks at the current ~1.18 km average.
