## What Comfortable Looks Like

The standing goal this area is working towards, held from the dissolved Self-Taught Photography project:

- Can explain the exposure triangle and how the three settings trade off against each other.
- Can set aperture, shutter speed, and ISO by hand for a given scene without falling back to auto.
- Has produced a set of manual-mode shots that turned out as intended.
- Can take a shot through a basic edit — exposure, color, crop — and explain why each adjustment was made.
- Has built a portfolio of the best shots, taken in manual mode and edited.

No finish line, which is why it lives here rather than in `projects`. Each finishable piece of it becomes a project in turn.

## Approach

Pick a place, search photos of it, and mimic them. Pick a style to study — street or portrait.

Photographers to study: Vivian Maier, Steve McCurry, Gregory Halpern.
