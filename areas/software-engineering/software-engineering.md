# Software Engineering

## Purpose

Maintain software engineering craft, delivery quality, development workflows, technical judgment, and reusable work patterns.

## Standard to Maintain

- Keep engineering workflows and checklists easy to reuse.
- Capture lessons from implementation, debugging, reviews, releases, and incidents.
- Convert concrete outcomes into projects when there is a finish line.
- Keep reusable references organized in [[resources/software-engineering/software-engineering|Software Engineering resources]].

## Review Rhythm

Review monthly, or during a weekly review when engineering work is under active pressure.
