# Parallel Park the Car

## Checklist

- [ ] Drive forward, keeping about 1 meter from the parked cars beside you.
- [ ] Skip the first open stall. Stop when your shoulder lines up with the middle of the second car.
- [ ] Shift into reverse, turn the wheel fully to the left.
- [ ] Release the clutch slowly. Watch the left side mirror and wheel to bring the car's left side into the stall with a 15-20 cm gap from the adjacent vehicle.
- [ ] Once the left side is positioned, switch focus to the right side mirror to confirm the tail clears the right side.
- [ ] Once parallel to the other cars, straighten the wheel.
- [ ] Back up slowly until your side mirrors line up with the adjacent vehicles' mirrors, then stop.

## Notes

- Covers backing into a parallel stall only. Garage/tight-space parking still needs its own pass if that keeps giving trouble.
