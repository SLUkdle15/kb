# Drive to Nam Dinh

Source: [[archives/drive-to-thien-truong/drive-to-thien-truong|Drive to Thien Truong]]

## Checklist

- [ ] Papers: driver's license (or VNeID), car registration, đăng kiểm in date, bảo hiểm TNDS bắt buộc in date, CCCD.
- [ ] ETC account topped up for the expressway tolls.
- [ ] Full tank before leaving.
- [ ] Phone mount, charger, and Maps set to the destination.
- [ ] Cash for parking, water and coffee, sunglasses.

## Notes

- Vành Đai 3 → Pháp Vân is the tricky part (noted after the first drive on 2026-10-10). Before leaving, look at the interchange in Maps, and follow the overhead "Pháp Vân – Cầu Giẽ / Ninh Bình" signs early instead of the last moment.
- QL21B / Liêm Tuyền – Nam Định road: not an expressway, so the general road limits apply unless signs say otherwise — outside built-up areas up to 90 km/h with a median, 80 km/h without; in built-up areas 60 km/h with a median, 50 km/h without. Watch for speed signs and cameras.
- Speed: Pháp Vân – Cầu Giẽ max 100 km/h; Cầu Giẽ – Ninh Bình max 120 km/h; minimum usually 60 km/h. Lane signs on overhead gantries override.
- Safe distance (dry road): over 60–80 km/h ≥ 55 m, 80–100 km/h ≥ 70 m, 100–120 km/h ≥ 100 m. Use the "Kiểm tra cự ly" marker signs; leave more in rain.
- Toilet stop: VEC rest stop at Km227 on Cầu Giẽ – Ninh Bình (Hà Nam), just past the Phú Thứ tunnel, before the Liêm Tuyền exit. Both directions, on the right side; follow the "Trạm dừng nghỉ" signs, not the Phú Thứ exit. Maps: search "Trạm dừng nghỉ Km227". Few official stops otherwise — go before leaving. Never stop on the emergency lane to pee.
- Bảo hiểm tự nguyện (vật chất, tai nạn) does not count as TNDS bắt buộc unless the certificate says "TNDS bắt buộc".
