# Transfer Money from TPBank to CIMB

Source: [[archives/transfer-money-to-mom/transfer-money-to-mom|Transfer Money to Mom]]

## Checklist

- [ ] Confirm the total amount and the recipient's CIMB account details.
- [ ] If the total is 10 billion VND or less: send it via the TPBank app using fast transfer — it auto-splits the transaction into 500 million VND chunks.
- [ ] If the total is more than 10 billion VND: fast-transfer the first 10 billion via the app, then visit a TPBank branch for the remainder.
- [ ] For the branch visit: bring your physical ID.
- [ ] At the branch: sign exactly as your signature registered with TPBank.
- [ ] Confirm the fee before executing (0.02%, capped at 1.1 million VND).
- [ ] Verify the money landed in the CIMB account.

## Notes

- Fee is 0.02% (e.g. 6 billion VND → 1.2 million), but the 1.1 million VND cap kicks in from 5.5 billion upward — so a 6 billion transfer costs **1.1 million VND**.
- Unclear whether the in-app fast transfers carry a fee, or whether the 0.02% applies only to the branch transfer — ask when at the branch.
