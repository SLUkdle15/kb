# Decide Whether to Cancel Weekly Soccer

Source: [[archives/next-actions/2026-07-28 - Find Out How to Put Soccer Protocol Into KB|Find Out How to Put Soccer Protocol Into KB]]

## Checklist

- [ ] By Friday: confirm headcount and decide whether to keep or cancel the field booking.
- [ ] If cancelling, notify the group and the field owner.
- [ ] Day before: pack gear — boots, shin guards, jersey, water.
- [ ] Pick 1–2 focus cues from [[resources/soccer/2026-08-04 - Soccer Playing Cues|Soccer Playing Cues]].

## Notes

- A weakness that keeps showing up in matches is the signal to edit this checklist or add a cue.
