## Action

Shutter speed and aperture are covered. Decide what to learn next on the body itself and turn it into its own action.

ISO is the obvious candidate — it is the last leg of the exposure triangle and still says "Not covered yet" in [[projects/self-taught-photography/2026-09-09 - Exposure Basics on the 6D Mark II|Exposure Basics on the 6D Mark II]]. Check the camera's menus and dials for what else is worth a pass — metering, focus modes, white balance — before committing.

Material to draw the concept from — three LinkedIn Learning courses:

- [Introduction to Photography](https://www.linkedin.com/learning/introduction-to-photography/introduction?u=556470434)
- [The Practicing Photographer](https://www.linkedin.com/learning/the-practicing-photographer/introducing-the-practicing-photographer?u=556470434)
- [Photography Foundations: Composition](https://www.linkedin.com/learning/photography-foundations-composition/welcome?u=556470434)
