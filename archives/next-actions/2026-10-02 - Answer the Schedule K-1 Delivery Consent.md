# Answer the Schedule K-1 Delivery Consent

Area: [[areas/personal-finance/personal-finance|Personal Finance]]

## Action

A request arrived addressed to Le Duc Khang: *Schedule K-1 Electronic Delivery Consent*. Work out what it belongs to before answering it.

A Schedule K-1 is a US form — it reports one partner's share of a partnership's income, and the consent only governs whether that form arrives by email instead of post. Nothing in this vault records a US partnership interest; [[next/maybe/2026-08-21 - Start Investing|Start Investing]] is still a maybe. So the first question is which of three this is:

- Something real and forgotten — an ESOP, a fund, or an account opened through work that does issue a K-1.
- A misdirected notice for a different Le Duc Khang.
- A phishing lure. Tax-document consent requests are a common one, and the payload is the login page behind the consent link.

Resolve it from the sender's own domain and an account you already hold, not from the link in the message. If it turns out to be real, consent is fine and takes a minute; if it is not, nothing is owed.

## Done When

The request is traced to a real account and answered, or identified as not yours and deleted.

Done 2026-10-08. Traced to a real account and answered — the K-1 is genuine, not misdirected and not a lure. So a US partnership interest does exist, which this vault had no record of; the account is not named here.
