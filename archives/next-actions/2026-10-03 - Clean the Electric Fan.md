# Clean the Electric Fan

Area: [[areas/family/family|Family]]
Due: 2026-10-03

## Action

Committed to Saturday 2026-10-03 on 2026-10-02, off the undated next-actions list where it had sat since 09-25.

Research how the fan comes apart before touching it, then clean it. Three things decide the method: how far the grille and blade come off without breaking the clips, whether the motor housing can be wiped wet or only dry, and what is safe on the blade finish.

Then do it — grille, blade, and the vents on the motor housing, which is where the dust that matters actually sits.

## Done When

The fan is clean and reassembled, running without wobble or rattle.
