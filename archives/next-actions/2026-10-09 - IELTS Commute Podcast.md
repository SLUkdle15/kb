## Action

10 min on the commute, before the 09:00 conference: an English podcast or video at natural speed, on something actually interesting. Pick **one phrase** and say it out loud.

The first trial of the low-effort daily input settled on 2026-10-08 — listening that does a small active thing, not background noise.

**What counts as a phrase:** a multi-word chunk a native speaker says as one unit, two to five words — a collocation (*draw customers away from*), a phrasal verb (*end up doing*), or a discourse move (*to be fair*, *it comes down to*, *for the most part*). Below sentence level on purpose: a whole sentence is a memorised answer, which [[projects/score-ielts-7-overall/speaking-6-5-levers|Speaking 6.5 Levers]] says earns nothing. A single rare word does not count either — the chunk is what carries the grammar around it.

Test it by saying it inside your own sentence, not the podcast's. If it survives that, and you would use it again in Part 1 or Part 3, it earns a line in [[projects/score-ielts-7-overall/vocabulary|Vocabulary]].

## Done When
