# Create a CIMB Bank Account for Dad

Area: [[areas/family/family|Family]]
Due: 2026-09-27

## Action

Open a CIMB account in Dad's name. Sit down with him, his phone and his ID card, and go through CIMB's sign-up together — the identity check needs his face and his ID, so he has to be there.

## Done When

Dad has an active CIMB account, can log in on his phone, and knows his account number.
