# Archives

## What Belongs Here

Archives hold inactive material you may want later.

Examples:

- Completed project folders
- Old versions of plans
- Outdated references
- Paused ideas
- Notes from responsibilities you no longer maintain

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Active project work
- Current areas of responsibility
- Reference material you still expect to use often
- Inbox items that have not been reviewed

## Review Frequency

Review only when needed.

Archives are designed to reduce clutter without deleting useful context.
