# Atomic Habit

- What is a habit, mechanically — is there a repeatable structure to it, and where does it tend to break?
- Is repetition alone enough to see myself change, or is something else needed?
- How do I build a habit that lasts, through bad days and not just good ones?
	- Having a reward system: is this defined or spontaneous?
    - How to optimize/ prime my env?
    - How to log my performance? and is this act as a craving factor?
- How do I remove a bad habit — specifically endless social media and poor morning focus?
- When a habit plateaus, what do I do?
