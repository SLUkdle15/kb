## Reading Questions

The roadmap's question for this phase is: where should the boundaries be?

- What decides where one bounded context ends and the next begins?
- What belongs inside an aggregate, and what does that boundary buy?
- How do domain events move information across a boundary without coupling the two sides?
- What is ubiquitous language for, beyond naming?
- How do you tell the core domain from the supporting ones, and what follows from that?
- When is an anti-corruption layer worth its cost?
