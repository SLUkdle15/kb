# Lunch Nap Plan

Source: [[inbox/2026-10-12 - The Power of Full Engagement|The Power of Full Engagement]]
Related: [[resources/energy-management/2026-10-14 - Building and Renewing Energy in Four Dimensions|Building and Renewing Energy in Four Dimensions]], [[resources/energy-management/2026-10-14 - Four Principles Behind Full Engagement|Four Principles Behind Full Engagement]]

## Idea

Cook tomorrow's lunch the night before, use the lunch break to nap, then eat afterwards.

## Adjustments From the Book

- **Don't let the gap get too long.** The book's rule is to eat small amounts every 2–3 hours. Pushing lunch past the nap can stretch the gap after breakfast to 5+ hours. Eat a small snack (fruit, nuts, yogurt) before the nap so you aren't napping hungry or waking up shaky.
- **Keep the nap short.** A short rest fits the early-afternoon dip and works like a recovery break, not a second night. About 20 minutes, with an alarm, so you don't wake up groggy.
- **Make the late lunch light.** A big, sugary or starchy meal after the nap brings back the slump the nap was meant to fix. Keep it moderate and low-sugar.
- **Don't let night cooking eat into sleep.** Night sleep matters most. Cook early in the evening, or cook in batches a few times a week, so bedtime doesn't move later. Don't snack on heavy food while cooking close to bed.

## Check

- If you need the nap every day just to get through the afternoon, fix night sleep first (same bedtime and wake time, enough hours) and don't rely on the nap.
