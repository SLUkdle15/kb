1. You must immediately transition from being the passer to being a passing option -> 3 actually steps --> further investigate
2. supporting target forward by  just slow down and hold your run and support underneath her so she can play the way she is facing. Give her an angle of support that is better than square. 
3. Inside foot = the foot closer to the center of the field. Outside foot = the foot closer to the touchline. It depends on how the target is facing when she receives the ball — not about which part of the foot strikes the ball.
4. Smart players make 90% of their decisions before the ball ever gets to them. constantly "checking shoulder" but also keep it simple ---> further investigate
5. One run for the defender’s benefit, one for mine. That means that once you determine where you want the ball, take your first two or three steps in the opposite direction. -- 2 runs
6. as a midfielder, you should absolutely look for the ball in behind sometimes, but _only when you have the time and space to actually execute it_.
7. The most dangerous pass in soccer is a penetrating ball that splits a pair of defenders, often referred to as a seam ball or a split.  the attacker will travel through one seam, the ball will travel through another, and they will meet up behind the defense.
8. no half clearance.
9. Don’t be ashamed to employ the toe-poke. 
10. DON'T TURN INTO PRESSURE: That will keep your body between the ball and your opponent, allowing you to protect the ball. when It’s about dribbling to escape pressure and maintain possession of the ball.
11. Smart players are habitually spying on the ball carrier’s eyes, looking for a
half-step head start.
