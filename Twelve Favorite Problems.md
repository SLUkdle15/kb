# Twelve Favorite Problems

These are open-ended questions I want my Second Brain to help me explore over time.

## My Current Questions

1. How can I use coding agents to build scalable, high-quality software faster without losing architectural control?
2. How can I become a world-class Solution Architect who designs systems, influences stakeholders, and creates business value?
3. How can I become the kind of person who naturally creates leadership, ownership, and momentum in every group I join?
4. How can I turn knowledge into mental models, systems, and daily behaviors that reliably improve my life and work?
5. How can I become financially competent and build a strong personal system for money, tax, law, property, renting, buying, and selling?
6. How can I design a personal growth system that makes lifelong learning and progress automatic?
7. How can I become an unstoppable competitor by mastering golf as my primary lifelong sport, using chess to sharpen my mind, and building the strength, mobility, and endurance to perform for decades?
8. How can I build an intelligence system that helps me spot important ideas, technologies, and opportunities early, then turn them into original insights, decisions, and action?

## How to Use This File

When capturing a new note, ask:

- Does this help me think about one of my favorite problems?
- Could this become useful for a current or future project?
- Is this connected to an area of responsibility?
- Is this a resource for later research?
