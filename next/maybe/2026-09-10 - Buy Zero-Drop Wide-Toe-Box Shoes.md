# Buy Zero-Drop Wide-Toe-Box Shoes

## Action

Buy a pair of shoes with a zero drop and a wide toe box. Candidates named so far: the Asics Gel-Kayano 14, or a Hoka.

Worth checking before spending anything: neither candidate actually meets the criteria. The Gel-Kayano 14 is a retro runner with a roughly 10 mm drop, and most Hokas sit around 4-5 mm — wide-fitting, but not zero drop. Brands that are genuinely zero drop with a wide toe box are Altra, Topo Athletic, Xero, and Vivobarefoot. So decide which matters more: the two shoes, or the two criteria.

A third candidate spotted on 2026-09-11: the Hoka Clifton 11 Wide (Giày Chạy Bộ Nam HOKA Clifton 11 Wide, grey). The Wide version answers the toe box, but the Clifton is a cushioned road runner with a real heel-to-toe drop, not a zero-drop shoe — check the listed drop before buying. It is the same trade-off as above: this one is comfort, not the criteria.

The Gel-Kayano 14 was already on the shortlist in [[archives/next-actions/2026-08-22 - Buy New Everyday Shoes|Buy New Everyday Shoes]], which closed on 2026-09-03 — check what happened there before re-opening it.

A pair is bought, or the idea is dropped and this note is archived.
