# Finish a Half Marathon

Area: [[areas/health/health|Health]]

## Action

Train for and finish a half marathon — 21.1 km, any race, no time target on the first one.

Parked rather than started, for one reason: the lower back. [[areas/health/health|Health]] exists to manage lower back pain, and half marathon training means three to four running sessions a week for twelve to sixteen weeks — the load that a stalled back does worst under. The lower back routine and its 2026-10-15 checkpoint were both dropped on 2026-10-02, so no review settles this any more. Revisit it at a weekly review instead.

The open questions before it becomes a project are: whether the back can take the load, which race and what date (the date is what makes the plan real), what the current honest running base is, and whether the plan includes [[areas/health/do-the-lower-back-routine|the lower back routine]] rather than ignoring the back.

## Done When

Parked. Promote to a project once the back looks able to take the load and a specific race and date are picked — a half marathon with a date is a multi-step outcome with a finish line, which is what a project is for.
