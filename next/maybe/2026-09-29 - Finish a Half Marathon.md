# Finish a Half Marathon

Area: [[areas/health/health|Health]]

## Action

Train for and finish a half marathon — 21.1 km, any race, no time target on the first one.

Parked rather than started, for one reason: the lower back. [[areas/health/health|Health]] exists to manage lower back pain, and half marathon training means three to four running sessions a week for twelve to sixteen weeks — the load that a stalled back does worst under. The twice-weekly lower back routine has a checkpoint on 2026-10-15; that review decides whether this is even a sensible thing to want.

If it survives that checkpoint, the open questions before it becomes a project are: which race and what date (the date is what makes the plan real), what the current honest running base is, and whether a plan gets built around the back routine rather than on top of it.

## Done When

Parked. Promote to a project once the lower back review clears it and a specific race and date are picked — a half marathon with a date is a multi-step outcome with a finish line, which is what a project is for.
