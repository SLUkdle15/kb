# Buy a Golf Bag to Go to the Range Alone

Area: [[areas/golf-training/golf-training|Golf Training]]
Project: [[projects/self-taught-golf/self-taught-golf|Self-Taught Golf]]

## Action

Buy a golf bag to carry the clubs. The point is independence: with my own bag I can go to the range by myself on a weekday, instead of only on the weekend sessions.

Decide what the bag has to do first — carry stand bag versus cart bag, how many clubs it has to hold, and whether it has to fit in the car — then shop.

## Done When

A bag is bought and the clubs live in it, and at least one weekday range session has happened off the back of it.
