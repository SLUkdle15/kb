# Go to the Van Gogh Timeless Exhibition

## Action

Go to the Van Gogh Timeless exhibition.

First check the run dates, venue, ticket price, and whether tickets must be booked ahead. Once a date is picked, move this to `next/calendar`.

## Done When

Run dates, venue, and ticket price are checked, and either a date is picked and this moves to `next/calendar`, or it is a decided no. Undecided by the time the exhibition closes counts as a no by default.
