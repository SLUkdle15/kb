# Check Her Potted Pine and See If It Can Be Saved

Area: [[areas/romantic-relationship/romantic-relationship|Romantic Relationship]]

## Action

Look the pine over and work out what is wrong: needle colour and drop, how wet the soil is a finger deep, whether the pot actually drains, whether the roots have filled it, and how much light it gets where it stands.

Then decide whether it is recoverable and what the one fix is — repot, water differently, move it, or cut losses.

If it is savable, the standing care habit in [[next/maybe/2026-09-06 - Put More Effort into Taking Care of the Tree|Put More Effort into Taking Care of the Tree]] is what keeps it alive afterwards.

## Done When

I know whether the pine can be saved, and either the fix is done or started, or I have told her it is not coming back.
