# Pick One YouTube Coach to Follow

Project: [[projects/self-taught-golf/self-taught-golf|Self-Taught Golf]]
Area: [[areas/golf-training/golf-training|Golf Training]]

## Action

Choose a single YouTube coach or channel to take swing instruction from, and take the reference swing from that same source so the instruction and the model agree.

Watch a few candidates on one topic — the grip, say — and pick the one that explains it in a way that makes sense, not the one with the most views. The point is to stop at one.

## Done When

One channel is chosen, and one reference swing from it is saved somewhere retrievable to compare films against.
