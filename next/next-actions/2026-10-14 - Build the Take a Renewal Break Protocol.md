## Draft

### Checklist

- [ ] Stop when 90–120 minutes of focused work are up, or sooner at the first sign of fog, restlessness, or irritability.
- [ ] Name which energy is low: body (tired, stiff, hungry), emotions (tense, short-tempered), or mind (can't focus, rereading lines).
- [ ] Pick a real recovery for that one:
  - Body: get up and move, drink water, eat a light snack.
  - Emotions: a few minutes with someone you like, or something you enjoy.
  - Mind: switch to a different kind of activity, or take a walk.
- [ ] Skip junk recovery: no scrolling, email, caffeine or sugar to push through.
- [ ] Keep it short: 5–15 minutes, away from the screen.
- [ ] Come back and start with one clear next step.

### Notes

- The signal is the trigger, not the clock. If you're still sharp at 120 minutes, the break can wait a little; if you're foggy at 60, take it.
- A break that leaves you more tired, like scrolling, didn't count. Take another one.
- If you need breaks constantly all day, check sleep first.
- Left out on purpose: spiritual recovery (reflection, values, serving others) doesn't fit a 10-minute break and belongs in an evening or weekly protocol.
