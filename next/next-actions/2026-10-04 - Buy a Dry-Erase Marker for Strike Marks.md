# Buy a Dry-Erase Marker for Strike Marks

Project: [[projects/self-taught-golf/self-taught-golf|Self-Taught Golf]]
Area: [[areas/golf-training/golf-training|Golf Training]]
For: [[next/calendar/2026-10-11 - Go to the Range and Run a Low-Point Drill Session|the 2026-10-11 range session]]

## Action

Buy a whiteboard (dry-erase) marker from a stationery shop — or foot powder spray from a pharmacy, whichever is passed first. Before Sunday 2026-10-11.

Scribbled on the clubface, it shows where each ball struck, so strike location finally gets recorded instead of guessed.

## Done When

The marker or spray is in the golf bag.
