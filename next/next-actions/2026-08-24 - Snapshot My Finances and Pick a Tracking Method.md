# Snapshot My Finances and Pick a Tracking Method

Area: [[areas/personal-finance/personal-finance|Personal Finance]]

## Action

One sitting, two halves. The snapshot first — the tracking method is unchoosable until there is something to track.

**Calculate the position (~40 min).** Write down, in one place:

- Every account and its balance — TPBank (including the 6-month term at 6.2% from [[next/waiting/2026-08-17 - TPBank Savings Term Matures|2026-08-16]]), CIMB, cash, anything else.
- Monthly income, net.
- Recurring monthly outflows, listed individually rather than as one lump — the individual lines are the only part that is actionable later.
- Any money owed in either direction.

That yields two numbers worth having: net position, and monthly surplus or deficit.

**Pick a method (~20 min, hard stop).** Choose one way to keep this current and set it up. Bound this deliberately: default to a plain spreadsheet with one row per month, and do not spend the evening comparing budgeting apps. The method that gets updated beats the method with better features — the same reason the [[projects/score-ielts-7-overall/error-log|error log]] is a table and not a system.

Two things to decide while the numbers are in front of you, not later:

- What counts as the emergency fund, and how many months of outflows it currently covers.
- How often this gets revisited. The area already says monthly, so match that unless there is a reason not to.
