# Find a Golf Schedule and Lesson for Beginners

Project: [[projects/self-taught-golf/self-taught-golf|Self-Taught Golf]]

## Action

Find a shooting/driving range with a beginner-friendly class or lesson, and settle on a schedule (day/time) to practice stance, grip, and swing.

## Done When

A range, a specific beginner lesson, and a schedule (day/time) are identified.
