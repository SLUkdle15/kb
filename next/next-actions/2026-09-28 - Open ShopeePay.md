# Open ShopeePay

Area: [[areas/personal-finance/personal-finance|Personal Finance]]

## Action

Open a ShopeePay wallet on the phone: sign up, clear the identity verification with an ID card, and link a bank account so it can be topped up.

Decide at the same time what it is for, so it does not become a fourth untracked pocket of money. The obvious fit is the spending bucket in [[areas/personal-finance/personal-finance|Personal Finance]] — the 1,700-2,000 that is not a fixed bill and not the transfer out.

## Done When

The wallet is active and verified, a bank account is linked, and it has a stated job in the monthly outflow picture.
