Project: [[projects/decide-masters-field-of-study/decide-masters-field-of-study|Decide Master's Field of Study]]
Area: [[areas/personal-development/personal-development|Personal Development]]

## Action

Write one sentence naming what the degree must give that the FPT job plus self-study cannot.

One sentence. Not a list, not a session — ten minutes at a desk. Everything else in the project waits behind this, because a field cannot be chosen before the thing it is supposed to buy is named.

If no sentence survives scrutiny, the honest answer may be "not a master's". Write that down instead and stop; [[projects/pursue-a-masters-degree/pursue-a-masters-degree|Pursue a Master's Degree]] then gets reconsidered rather than continued.

## Done When

The sentence is written into [[projects/decide-masters-field-of-study/decide-masters-field-of-study|the project note]] — or the note records that no such sentence holds.
