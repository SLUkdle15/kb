# Stretch for Lower Back Pain After Heavy Soccer Sessions

Area: [[areas/soccer/soccer|Soccer]]

## Action

Do a lower-back stretch routine after any heavy soccer session, not just when pain is already bad — treat it as standard cooldown, not injury response.

## Done When

A short stretch routine (e.g. cat-cow, child's pose, seated spinal twist, hip flexor stretch) is done and lower back tension is noticeably reduced.
