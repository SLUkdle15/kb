# Find Her a Running Shoe

Area: [[areas/romantic-relationship/romantic-relationship|Romantic Relationship]]

## Action

**Size 39–40 (EU).** She sits between the two, so the pair gets tried on rather than ordered to a number. For running specifically, take the larger end — feet swell over distance and the toes need room in front, so a shoe that fits exactly while standing still is short once she is moving.

Settle what it is for, because it picks the shoe: actual running, or the [[areas/ho-tay-walking/ho-tay-walking|Hồ Tây]] walks, which are walking and want a different sole and less heel drop.

If it is a gift rather than something she asked for, record what she thinks of it afterwards — the standing rule on [[areas/romantic-relationship/2026-08-29 - Gift Ideas for Her|Gift Ideas for Her]].

## Done When

A pair is chosen and bought, in her size.
