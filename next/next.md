# Next

Use `next` for commitments and possible actions that are not yet project notes, area notes, or reusable resources.

## Sections

- [[next/next-actions/next-actions]] for concrete actions to do soon.
- [[next/calendar/calendar]] for date-specific or time-specific commitments.
- [[next/maybe/maybe]] for someday/maybe ideas and possible future projects.
- [[next/waiting/waiting]] for things blocked by someone or something else.

## Promotion Rule

Promote an item to `projects` when it becomes a multi-step outcome with a finish line.

Move context and learning into `areas` or `resources` when it becomes ongoing or reusable.

## Agent Workflow

Use the `next` agent skill to add small commitments.

Routing rule:

1. Specific date or time -> `next/calendar`
2. Someday/maybe or optional -> `next/maybe`
3. Blocked by someone or something -> `next/waiting`
4. Otherwise -> `next/next-actions`
