Area: [[areas/personal-finance/personal-finance|Personal Finance]]

## Action

The TPBank 6-month term at 6.2%, opened 2026-08-16, matures **2027-02-16** — see [[archives/next-actions/2026-08-16 - Register for a Savings Program|Register for a Savings Program]].

Decide before maturity, not at it: roll over, move to a better rate, or withdraw. Check whether TPBank auto-renews at the same rate, and compare against BIDV first. A term that auto-renews the day it matures takes the decision away.

This note is dated a week early on purpose. Re-date it if the maturity date moves.
