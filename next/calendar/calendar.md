# Calendar

Use this folder for date-specific or time-specific commitments.

## Belongs Here

- Tasks that must happen on a specific date.
- Event preparation notes.
- Time-bound reminders.

## Does Not Belong Here

- Someday ideas.
- General next actions without a real date.
- Project plans.
