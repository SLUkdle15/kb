# Repot the Garden Plants Out of Garden Soil

Area: [[areas/family/family|Family]]
Due: 2027-01-10

## Action

Repot all four potted plants — lemon, fig, dạ quỳnh, rose — out of the garden soil they are in now and into free-draining potting mix, opened up with perlite or coarse sand. Cactus or orchid mix for the dạ quỳnh.

Garden soil compacts a little more with every watering until there is no air left for the roots. January is the window: after the winter rest, before the spring flush, and ahead of nồm. Figures and mix per plant in [[resources/home-maintenance/2026-10-06 - Garden Plants|Garden Plants]].

Note each pot's size and material while they are out.

## Done When

All four are in free-draining mix, and the pot sizes are written into [[resources/home-maintenance/2026-10-06 - Garden Plants|Garden Plants]].
