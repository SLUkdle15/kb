# Weekly Tuesday Soccer

Every: Tuesday 17:30
Remind: 4
Protocol: [[areas/soccer/decide-whether-to-cancel-weekly-soccer|Decide Whether to Cancel Weekly Soccer]]

## Action

Play soccer every Tuesday at 17:30. The reminder fires 4 days before each match (Friday) — decide by then whether to cancel the field booking for that week.

## Done When

Recurring — stays here while the weekly match is on. Retire the note if the weekly booking stops.
