## Action

An hour at the range from 10:00, 7 iron throughout so it compares against 9/20 on 2026-10-04. A new drill replaces most of the hour; the tally shrinks to a 20-ball check at the end.

1. **Warm-up, 10 balls.** Half swings, no counting.
2. **Towel drill, 3 sets of 10.** Lay a folded towel about a hand-width behind the ball. Half to three-quarter swings; a ball counts only if the club misses the towel and the strike feels clean. Write the count per set.
3. **Contact check, 20 balls.** Full swings, no towel. Mark the clubface with the dry-erase marker or foot powder spray first ([[next/next-actions/2026-10-04 - Buy a Dry-Erase Marker for Strike Marks|Buy a Dry-Erase Marker for Strike Marks]]), then read the ball mark after each shot and re-mark. Write clean contact, then a tick per ball for strike (thin / fat / toe / heel) and flight (left / straight / right) as you go — not afterwards.
4. **Film 3-5 swings** on the tripod, down-the-line.

## Done When

The three towel-drill counts and the 20-ball check (clean contact, strike ticks, flight ticks) are written down the same day.
