# Pursue a Master's Degree

## Outcome

Be accepted into and enrolled in a master's degree program. Field of study is not yet decided — narrowing it down is part of this project.

## Constraints

Decided 2026-08-17:

- **Full-time.** Studying alongside the FPT job is out, so this becomes a resignation-or-leave decision at enrollment.
- **Abroad, in the US.** Adds a visa track (F-1) on top of admissions, and makes an English test mandatory.
- **Budget is not a limiting factor.** Cost does not filter the shortlist in step 3, and funding is not a prerequisite for applying.

## Deadline

Target: the Fall 2027 intake. US applications for that cycle typically close between December 2026 and February 2027, so the working deadline is **December 2026** — about four months out. Confirm the real per-program dates in step 4.

## Definition of Done

Enrolled in a master's program.

## Steps

Work these in order. Only the current step becomes a note in `next/next-actions`; promote the following one when it is done.

1. **Decide the field of study.** Narrow to one or two fields worth two years of effort. Everything downstream depends on this.
2. ~~**Set the constraints.**~~ Done 2026-08-17 — see Constraints above.
3. **Shortlist three to five US programs** that fit the field, spread across reach and safety.
4. **Check entrance requirements per shortlisted program.** TOEFL or IELTS, GRE if the program still requires it, transcripts, recommendation letters, statement of purpose, and each program's application deadline.
5. **Book the required tests.** TOEFL/IELTS slots fill months ahead and scores take weeks to report, so book as soon as step 4 names the test — this is the step most likely to blow the December deadline.
6. **Gather the documents.** Transcripts, degree certificate, CV, and any translation or notarization the programs require.
7. **Ask for recommendation letters.** Give referees at least a month and a copy of the statement of purpose.
8. **Write the statement of purpose**, then adapt it per program.
9. **Submit the applications** ahead of each deadline from step 4.
10. **Handle the outcome.** Accept an offer, request the I-20, apply for the F-1 visa, resign or arrange leave from FPT, enroll.

## Notes

Field is still open, which is why step 1 comes before any program research. Steps 5 through 8 can overlap once step 4 has produced concrete deadlines.

Going full-time in the US means the timeline is driven by two clocks that are not the application deadline: the English test (book early, scores take weeks) and the F-1 visa interview after admission. Budget not being a constraint removes the funding search but not these.
