# Learn Photo Basics

## Outcome

Learn enough photography basics to take better everyday and travel photos using both a camera and a phone, then create a simple personal shooting checklist.

## Deadline

No deadline.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] Understand exposure triangle: aperture, shutter speed, and ISO.
- [ ] Understand focus, light, and basic composition.
- [ ] Learn practical differences between shooting with a camera and a phone.
- [ ] Take 20 practice photos across camera and phone.
- [ ] Pick 5 best photos and write what worked.
- [ ] Create a one-page photo checklist for future use.
