# Projects

## What Belongs Here

Projects are active efforts with a specific outcome and a finish line.

Examples:

- Ship a software feature
- Prepare a solution architecture proposal
- Build a personal finance tracker
- Write a presentation
- Complete a learning sprint

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Ongoing responsibilities without an end date
- General research topics
- Completed projects
- Notes saved only because they are interesting

## Review Frequency

Review active projects weekly.

During review, ask:

- [ ] Is this still active?
- [ ] What is the next action?
- [ ] What note, packet, or decision would move this forward?
- [ ] Should this project be completed, paused, or archived?
