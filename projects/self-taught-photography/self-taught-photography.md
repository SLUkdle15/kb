# Self-Taught Photography

## Outcome

Comfortable shooting in manual mode and editing the results, setting exposure by hand and knowing why.

## Definition of Done

- Can explain the exposure triangle and how the three settings trade off against each other.
- Can set aperture, shutter speed, and ISO by hand for a given scene without falling back to auto.
- Has produced a set of manual-mode shots that turned out as intended.
- Can take a shot through a basic edit — exposure, color, crop — and explain why each adjustment was made.
- Has built a portfolio of the best shots, taken in manual mode and edited.

## Notes

Approach: pick a place, search photos of it, and mimic them — places already scouted are in [[areas/photography/2026-09-08 - Places I Have Looked At|Places I Have Looked At]]. Pick a style to study — street or portrait.

Photographers to study: Vivian Maier, Steve McCurry, Gregory Halpern.

Course plan — three LinkedIn Learning courses, in this order:

1. [Introduction to Photography](https://www.linkedin.com/learning/introduction-to-photography/introduction?u=556470434) — the camera and the exposure triangle.
2. [The Practicing Photographer](https://www.linkedin.com/learning/the-practicing-photographer/introducing-the-practicing-photographer?u=556470434) — shooting habits and working through real situations.
3. [Photography Foundations: Composition](https://www.linkedin.com/learning/photography-foundations-composition/welcome?u=556470434) — framing and seeing.

Each course gets its own action when the one before it is done.

What the camera itself has taught so far — modes, shutter speed, and the rest of the exposure triangle as it gets covered — is in [[projects/self-taught-photography/2026-09-09 - Exposure Basics on the 6D Mark II|Exposure Basics on the 6D Mark II]].
