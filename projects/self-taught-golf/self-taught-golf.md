## Notes

No formal lessons. The beginner-lesson search closed 2026-09-04 in favor of self-teaching from the range, run as [[areas/golf-training/run-a-beginner-range-session|Run a Beginner Range Session]].

Self-teaching loop: film the swing on the phone, compare it against one reference swing, and let the gap between the two pick the next thing to work on. One reference swing and one teacher to follow, not a list — averaging contradictory sources is the way this goes nowhere.

Interim, until there is anything to film with: run a contact tally instead. Over 20 balls with one club, record how many made clean contact, where on the face the strike lands (toe, heel, thin, fat), and which way the ball tends to go. Three numbers, no camera, and still a real before.
