# Home Swing Camera Setup

Project: [[projects/self-taught-golf/self-taught-golf|Self-Taught Golf]]
Area: [[areas/golf-training/golf-training|Golf Training]]

GoPro on its tripod at home. Down-the-line worked out 2026-10-03, face-on worked out and filmed by 2026-10-11.

1. Down the line: camera 2 m away, tripod about 1 m high — extend exactly the last 3 leg sections.
2. Face-on: same 1 m tripod height. Aim at the middle of the stance and keep the feet, ball, and ground in frame. Shoot vertical for the swing, horizontal for the ball.
