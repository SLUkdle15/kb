# Error Log

The most load-bearing note in the project. Band 7 grammar is defined as *frequent error-free sentences*, and the fastest way to produce more of them is to stop making the same five mistakes rather than to learn new structures.

## Rules

- **Repeated errors only.** A one-off slip is noise. An error logs here the second time it appears.
- **One line per error**, with a wrong version and a right version. Rules in the abstract do not transfer; corrected sentences do.
- **Add during the self-scoring pass**, not while writing. Hunting errors mid-essay wrecks timing.
- **Delete an entry once it stops appearing** in two consecutive weeks. A log that only grows stops being read.
- **Re-read it before every writing session and every recording**, and daily during taper week.
