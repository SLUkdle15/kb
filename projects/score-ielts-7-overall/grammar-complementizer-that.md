# Missing Complementizer "That"

Check a sentence against this before moving to the next one. Distilled from [[projects/score-ielts-7-overall/error-log|the error log]] — dropped 5 times across the two 2026-09-05 essays, the single most-repeated slip right now.

## Quick Cards

| Pattern | ❌ | ✅ | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Noun + defining clause | "a school of thought a written exam is not the best way" | "a school of thought **that** a written exam is not the best way" | The clause defines the noun (*namely that...*) rather than describing something else — needs *that*. |
| *is/was* + full clause | "one advantage is this format testing the knowledge" | "one advantage is **that** this format tests the knowledge" | When *be* is followed by a whole clause (subject + verb), not just a noun phrase, *that* is required. |
| *agree with the [noun]* | "I agree the argument written exams are not..." | "I agree **with** the argument **that** written exams are not..." | *agree* needs *with* before a noun object, and the noun still needs *that* before its defining clause. |

## The Test

Can the sentence be rephrased as "X, namely that..."? If the clause explains what the noun *is* rather than modifying some other subject, `that` is mandatory — this is a noun complement clause, not a relative clause, and the two look alike but behave differently.

- ❌ "a disadvantage is a student with social anxiety...may know the material" → ✅ "a disadvantage is **that** a student with social anxiety...may know the material"
- ❌ "There is a school of thought a written exam is not the best way" → ✅ "There is a school of thought **that** a written exam is not the best way"

Scan habit: after writing a sentence with *is/are/argue/believe/agree* + a noun like *fact, argument, belief, idea, advantage, disadvantage, possibility, school of thought*, check whether a full clause follows. If it does, `that` goes right before it.
