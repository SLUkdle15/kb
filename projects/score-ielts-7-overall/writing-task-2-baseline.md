## Prompt

International tourism has brought enormous benefit to many places. At the same time, there is concern about its impact on local inhabitants and the environment. Do the disadvantages of international tourism outweigh the advantages?

## Essay

*(paste the timed 40-minute essay here, as written — no edits after the clock stops)*

Tourist services brings up numerous benefits over the year for countries such as: the financial income but also harm the its environment. However, i believe that the advantages cant be outweighed by the disadvantages.

First of all, the financial gain can be reinvested to improve the inhabitants as well as the environment. For example, VN is heavily depend on tourism as the field is one of the main contributor of its economics. The government can distribute financial resources to pay for new infra or equipment. For example: the beaches are cleared of trash and also coral species in island are revived with protected environment.  At the same time, the money can be used to run campaigns make tourists as well as locals aware to preserve nature.

Second, tourism as a field can make the environmental issues become more aware by global audiences. This can attract researchers or donors or journalists who concern about a country problem and open the door to solve or give a helping hand. For example, VN wild life is worse every year. Statically wild life animals are less and less being seen like bear, tiger. This could be the results of over modernization and the issue can be addressed via social platform shared by tourists that come to VN. This make the government become aware of the issues and therefore they will take action.

In conclusion, even though tourism often damage on a country environment, the benefits it create is undeniably impactful. With the financial gain and the ability to address the issues of environment, I believe its advantages certainly outweighs it disadvantages.
