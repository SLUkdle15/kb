## Prompt

The chart below shows the percentage of households with internet access in four countries — Vietnam, South Korea, Brazil, and Nigeria — between 2000 and 2020.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

| Year | Vietnam | South Korea | Brazil | Nigeria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2000 | 1% | 45% | 3% | 0% |
| 2005 | 13% | 78% | 21% | 1% |
| 2010 | 31% | 90% | 41% | 6% |
| 2015 | 53% | 95% | 58% | 16% |
| 2020 | 70% | 97% | 74% | 35% |

Source: invented — no Cambridge past paper book available. Swap in a real Cambridge 16–20 Task 1 prompt here if the books become available.

## Essay

From the chart above. the percentage of family have internet  access in Nigeria is the lowest while south Korea has the highest number from 2000 to 2020. And 2000 always has the lowest percentages across 5 years.

In 2000, South Korea already has approximately 50% of household can access online while the others 3 rarely have any. In 2020, again South Korea has almost all of its household can access to the internet, compare to the lowest which is Nigeria with 35%.

Both Brazil and VN has a very steady trend of house hold user across 5 years. Brazil started with 3% in 2000, and almost one fifth of its house hold has internet access 5 year later. and finally end up with 74% second highest among 4 countries in 2020. Vn show a similarly trend across the board.

Lastly, Nigeria has the lowest percentage of household user in 2020.

### The Pass

- **No overview sentence.** Paragraph 1 tries to double as intro and overview but never states the 2–3 main trends before the detail starts. Caps Task Achievement at 5 on its own.
- **Selection, not listing — partly there.** Extremes (South Korea highest, Nigeria lowest) and the Brazil/VN steady-growth pairing are the right features to pick. But the closing paragraph restates Nigeria's 2020 low a third time instead of adding anything new — a wasted sentence, not a selection failure.
- **Error-free sentences: roughly 1 of 10, about 10 percent.** Worse than the Task 2 baseline's 13 percent. Grammar is the binding constraint here too.
- **New pattern not seen in the Task 2 baseline: present tense for past chart data.** *"South Korea already has approximately 50%"*, *"almost one fifth... has internet access"* — should be *had*. Appears three times in this one essay, which is enough to flag even on a first sighting.
- **Word count.** ~166, clears the 150 minimum.

### What to Fix First

1. **Write the overview, second sentence, every time.** Non-negotiable per the plan — the highest-yield single fix available on Task 1.
2. **Past tense for historical data.** A Task 1 chart covering 2000–2020 describes what already happened; present tense reads as a new, repeated error type.
3. **Subject-verb agreement and plural -s**, same categories confirmed again from the Task 2 baseline — see [[projects/score-ielts-7-overall/error-log|Error Log]].
4. **Cut the redundant closing sentence.** If a final paragraph doesn't add a feature, it shouldn't exist — end on the last comparison instead.
