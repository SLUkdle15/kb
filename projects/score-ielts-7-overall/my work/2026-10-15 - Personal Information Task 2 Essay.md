## Prompt

The personal information of many individuals is held by large internet companies and organizations. Do you think the advantages of this outweigh the disadvantages?

## Essay

Many people continue to give large organizations their personal information. This action can cost people to get their identities exposed, but theses companies are best to put their personal information into good uses and because the identities has stolen cannot be undone, the disadvantage are greater of the two.

These firm can offer more personalized and efficient services by using their customer data. Most streaming platforms nowadays draw customers by aiding them to find new movies based on customer watching history. Example in Vietnam most people use Netflix for online movie streaming because they can find new movies using the recommendation lists on the dashboard. The costumers therefore save time on using the services and they willing to ignore despite knowing their data is being used.
