## Feedback

- **Advantage is a property, not an effect.** *Builds up experiences* can't be denied. Name a person and what changes: someone who leaves a secure job to start a business learns skills and finds out what work suits them.
- **Disadvantage is the definition of risk.** *Losing something we have* describes every risk. Friendship is concrete but needs a mechanism (moving away for a riskier job).
- **Ground doesn't fit.** A lost friendship is permanent too, so permanence can't decide it. Either switch the disadvantage to money (it comes back, skills last) or keep friendship and use remedy.
- **Opener drops half the prompt.** Add *in their careers as well as their private lives*.
- **Sentence 2 has no ground clause.** Model: *Taking a risk can cost people years of savings, but it also teaches them skills and shows them what they are capable of, and because money can be earned back while that knowledge lasts a lifetime, the advantages are the greater of the two.*
- **Language:** *people who taking* → *who take* (twice); *had built up* → *have built*; spelling *outweigh*, *fields*, *permanence*.
