# Book 16 Test 2 Task 2 Essay

In their advertising, businesses nowadays usually emphasise that their products are new in some way. Why is this? Do you think it is a positive or negative development?

Businesses promote newness mainly because when rival products having the same functionalities, being new is the one difference they can claim. A buyers comparing two phones with similar features has no reason to switch brands, but a new feature from the other brand will give them one. For example, Xiaomi has drawn customers away from Apples by ad features the Iphone did not yet offer. Once one company wins buyers this way. its rival are forces to answer with novelties of their own which is why almost every ad now lead with what is new.

However, this practice persuades people to replace product that still work perfectly. 
Mechanism; Advertising that celebrates the latest model quietly make the current one outdated 
Example: Every September, Apple promotes a new iPhone whose main changes are a slightlybetter camera and a new colour, yet millions of people trade in phones only a year or two old.
Consequence: The buyer pays hundreds of dollars for an improvement they will barely notice, spending on the feeling of being up to date rather than on any real need.
