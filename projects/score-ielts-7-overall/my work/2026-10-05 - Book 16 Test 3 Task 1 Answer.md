## Clean Version

This uses only the facts in the answer. No locations were added because the map was not to hand, which is why it is still short.

> The maps show an airport as it is now and the changes planned for it next year.
>
> Overall, the gate area will be enlarged and the arrivals hall will gain a new facility, while the other areas will keep their size but receive new amenities.
>
> The most significant change is that the concourse will be rebuilt in a V shape, which will allow an additional ten gates to be added. The concourse will also include a train system to carry passengers between the gates. The only other change in terms of space is in the arrivals hall, where a car hire area will be introduced.
>
> The remaining areas will stay the same size, but they will include new facilities. The departures hall will include a bag drop, and the empty space beyond security control will be filled with shops.
