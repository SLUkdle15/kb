# Path from 5 to 7

Distilled from the self-score of the [[projects/score-ielts-7-overall/practice-essays|2026-08-31 Task 1 practice]]. Grammar and Lexical Resource are the binding constraint, not structure — matches the project's own [[projects/score-ielts-7-overall/score-ielts-7-overall|Baseline Verdict]].

## 1. Grammar — the biggest lever (0% error-free on the 08-31 essay)

Two of the project's four mechanical repeat categories hit again today:

- **Articles before singular countable nouns** — "an average," "a vacuum cleaner," "a refrigerator." A 6+ time repeat now.
- **Don't drop the head noun** — say what's being counted: "the number of **hours** spent on housework," not "the number of doing housework."

These are mechanical, not fluency — the fast fix, unlike raw fluency. Master four clean structures and reuse them everywhere: a defining relative clause, a conditional, a passive for processes, a contrast clause with *while/whereas*. A short correct sentence beats a long broken one.

## 2. Lexical Resource — flat at 5

- Swap imprecise collocations for exact ones: "dropped massively" → *fell sharply* / *declined steadily*. "Subsided" is the wrong register for a shrinking number.
- Build a small reusable set: *rose, climbed, increased steadily* / *fell, declined, dropped sharply*. Add corrected versions into [[projects/score-ielts-7-overall/repertoire|Repertoire]] so they get reused, not just corrected once.

## 3. Task Achievement — one sentence from 7

State the **relationship between the two charts** explicitly on a mixed-chart task — that's what separates a 6 from a 7: *"As ownership of household appliances rose, the time spent on housework fell correspondingly."* Had all the data for this on 08-31 and never said it.

## 4. Coherence — kill the mechanical linkers

Replace "First,"/"Second," with referencing: *"This growth was mirrored by a steady decline in..."*

## Bottom Line

Structure (overview, length) gets Task Achievement and Coherence to ~6 alone. Getting Grammar and Lexical off 5 is what actually moves the overall band, and it's the slow one — so the highest-value habit is running the full 20-minute self-scoring pass after every essay, not skipping it under time pressure, specifically counting error-free sentences and catching these categories before they repeat again.
