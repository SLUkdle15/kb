# Learn Photography

## Outcome

Comfortable shooting in auto mode, understanding what the camera is doing.

## Definition of Done

- Can explain the exposure triangle and how the three settings trade off against each other.
- Understands what the camera is choosing (aperture, shutter speed, ISO) when shooting in auto mode and why.
- Has produced a set of auto-mode shots that turned out as intended.
