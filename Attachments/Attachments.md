# Attachments

## What Belongs Here

Use this folder for files attached to notes.

Examples:

- Images
- PDFs
- Exports
- Diagrams
- Screenshots
- Audio or video files referenced by notes

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Standalone notes
- Project briefs
- Templates
- Files that should live outside the vault

## Review Frequency

Review monthly or when the vault becomes heavy.

Remove unused attachments when you are confident no notes link to them.
