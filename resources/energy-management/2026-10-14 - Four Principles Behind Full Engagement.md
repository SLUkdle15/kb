# Four Principles Behind Full Engagement

## Core Idea

1. Full engagement requires four sources of energy: physical, emotional, mental, and spiritual — see [[resources/energy-management/2026-10-14 - Building and Renewing Energy in Four Dimensions|Building and Renewing Energy in Four Dimensions]].
2. Because energy capacity can be drained by both overuse and underuse, we must balance energy expenditure with rest.
3. To build capacity, we must push beyond our limits.
4. To keep the model going, we need to build positive energy routines.
