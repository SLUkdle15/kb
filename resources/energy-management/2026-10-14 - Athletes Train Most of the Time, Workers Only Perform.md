# Athletes Train Most of the Time, Workers Only Perform

## Core Idea

Professional athletes:

1. Spend about 90% of their time training and only 10% performing.
2. Have an off-season of around 5 months a year.
3. Have a career span of about 10 years.

Most knowledge workers do the reverse: they perform most of the day, almost never train or recover, get no off-season, and are expected to keep it up for a 40-year career.

## Questions This Answers

- Why does sustained high performance at work feel unsustainable?
- What would "training" and "off-season" mean outside of sport?
