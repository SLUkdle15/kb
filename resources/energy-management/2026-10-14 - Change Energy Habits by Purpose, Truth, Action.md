# Change Energy Habits by Purpose, Truth, Action

## Core Idea

A three-step model for changing how you manage energy:

1. **Define purpose** — what matters most and why.
2. **Face the truth** — how you are actually spending your energy now.
3. **Take action** — build routines that close the gap.
