# Building and Renewing Energy in Four Dimensions

## Core Idea

Capacity grows when we push our muscles past their limits and then rest. Each dimension needs its own kind of recovery.

|   | Physical | Emotional | Mental | Spiritual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Capacity looks like** | Stamina, fitness, quick recovery | Staying positive and calm under pressure | Sustained, flexible focus | Motivation and commitment that lasts |
| **Key muscles** | Endurance, strength, flexibility | Self-confidence, self-control, social skills, empathy | Mental preparation, visualization, positive self-talk, time management, creativity | Character (living by your values) |
| **Rest and renewal** | Sleep, healthy snacks every 2–3 hours, water, movement, breaks every 90–120 minutes | Enjoyable, fulfilling activities and time with people you care about | Changing channels: switching to a different kind of activity, walks, exercise, sleep | Reflection, time spent on your values, serving others |
| **Junk recovery to avoid** | Caffeine and sugar to push through fatigue | Hours of passive TV or compulsive gaming | Scrolling social media or checking email on breaks | Busyness that leaves no time to reflect |

## Questions This Answers

- Which kind of energy am I short on, and what would build it?
- How do I rest each kind of energy, and am I using real recovery or junk recovery?
