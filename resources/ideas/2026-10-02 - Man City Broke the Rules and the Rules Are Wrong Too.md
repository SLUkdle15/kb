# Man City Broke the Rules and the Rules Are Wrong Too

From a Mark Goldbridge take on the Man City case. Two separate wrongs; admitting one does not settle the other.

- **City cheated.** They broke the rules to buy power, and "everyone would have done the same" does not clear them.
- **The rules protect incumbents.** Spending caps tied to a club's own revenue let the biggest earners spend most, so a smaller club can never outspend its way up. That is a moat with a fairness label on it.

Open question: what rule stops a club buying a title without guaranteeing the same six finish on top? An absolute hard cap? Redistributed TV money?
