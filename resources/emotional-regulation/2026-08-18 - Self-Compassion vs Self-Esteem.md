# Self-Compassion vs Self-Esteem

## Core Idea

Insecurity, anxiety, and depression are incredibly common in our society, much of it driven by self-judgment — beating ourselves up when we feel we aren't winning in the game of life. Self-compassion provides the same benefits as high self-esteem without its drawbacks: a way to stop judging and evaluating ourselves altogether, rather than a way to win the evaluation.

## Useful Details

Compassion involves the recognition and clear seeing of suffering, and feelings of kindness for those who are suffering, so that the desire to help — to ameliorate suffering — emerges. Compassion to ourselves first requires that we stop to recognize our own suffering. Everyone is worthy of compassion.

Three components, which are what the self-compassion mantra rehearses:

1. Mindfulness — noticing that this is a moment of suffering.
2. Common humanity — suffering is part of life.
3. Kindness — may I be kind to myself in this moment.
