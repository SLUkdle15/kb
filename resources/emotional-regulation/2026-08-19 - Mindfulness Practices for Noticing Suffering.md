# Mindfulness Practices for Noticing Suffering

## Core Idea

Self-compassion starts with recognizing that we are suffering — we can't heal what we can't feel. These two practices train the noticing reflex outside of triggered moments, so it is available inside them.

## Mindfulness in Daily Life

- Pick one everyday activity (walking, brushing teeth, eating breakfast) and do it with full attention
- Notice raw sensations as they happen — feet on the ground, air temperature, muscles moving — without labeling or analyzing
- When your mind wanders, gently bring it back to the sensation
- Goal: stay immersed in the present moment, using ordinary daily tasks as practice, so you build the habit of being present rather than autopiloting through life

## Noting Practice

- Sit still, eyes closed, 10-20 minutes
- Whatever arises — a sound, an itch, a thought, an emotion — quietly label it ("hearing," "itching," "planning," "anxiety") then let it go and move to the next thing
- If you drift into thought for a while, just note "lost in thought" and return
- Goal: train quick, non-judgmental awareness of _anything_ passing through your mind, building the reflex of noticing before getting swept away
