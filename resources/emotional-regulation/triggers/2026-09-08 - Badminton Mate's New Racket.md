# Badminton Mate's New Racket

My badminton mate tells me he is getting a new racket -> I feel bad.

Not comparison. What I feel is jealousy: he has something he is really into. I still play with my old racket, and not because I don't have the money.

My first answer was that I have my own thing, it just isn't badminton. But really — right now there is nothing I am really into.

## Reframe

Nothing to do here, and it is a good thing. He likes the sport and he is sticking with it, and it means I have a mate to play with.
