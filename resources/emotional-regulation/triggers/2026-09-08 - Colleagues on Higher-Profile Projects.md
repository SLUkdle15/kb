# Colleagues on Higher-Profile Projects

My colleagues get staffed on projects better than mine, working with a lot of high-level executives -> I feel shit.

What I feel is being outranked, and the thing I am actually measuring is exposure: who is in the room with the executives. That is the engine running a comparison and turning on the warning light — [[resources/emotional-regulation/2026-08-18 - The Rank-Monitoring Engine Behind Self-Criticism|The Rank-Monitoring Engine Behind Self-Criticism]].

The story my mind makes is that the better project is a verdict: they were picked because they are better engineers, and I was not considered. Stated plainly it does not hold — staffing runs on timing, availability, and who already knew the stakeholder. It is also a fixed self-label, so restate it as a conditional: not "I am behind" but "I am not on this project, this time" ([[resources/emotional-regulation/2026-08-19 - Common Humanity Loosens Fixed Self-Labels|Common Humanity Loosens Fixed Self-Labels]]).

What I need is to know the work I do is seen, and a concrete route to the kind of project I want.

## Reframe

Wanting that exposure is not jealousy to talk myself out of — it is information about what I want next, and worth taking seriously. What does not follow is the ranking. Wanting the room is a goal; concluding I do not deserve it is the engine talking.

The route already exists and I am on it: [[areas/technical-growth/2026-06-03 - Software Architect Roadmap|Software Architect Roadmap]]. The wise action is to make the work visible to the people who staff these projects, not to earn the room by feeling bad about being outside it.
