# Capture What Resonates

## Core Idea

Capture only what may help future me think, decide, create, debug, explain, or remember.

## Capture From External Sources

- Guides
- Books
- Videos
- Podcasts
- Articles
- Documentation

## Capture From Internal Sources

- Own ideas
- Insights
- Questions
- Decisions
- Mistakes
- Personal patterns

## For External Sources, Capture

- One-sentence summary
- Top ideas
- What resonated
- What I will apply
- My own insight
- Questions or disagreements

## Reminder

Do not worry about missing every key point. The goal is to help future me think and act, not preserve the whole source.
