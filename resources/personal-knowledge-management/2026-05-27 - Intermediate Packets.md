# Intermediate Packets

## Core Idea

Break bigger projects into small reusable pieces of output.

## A Packet Should

1. Be concrete output.
2. Be reusable later.
3. Move a bigger project forward.
4. Be easy for future me to understand.
5. Be finished enough to use.

## Storage Rule

Store packets where they will be used next:

- Projects
- Areas
- Resources
- Archive
