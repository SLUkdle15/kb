# Distill Notes

## Core Idea

Turn raw notes into actionable, bite-sized summaries.

## Process

1. Capture in bullets first.
2. Later split strong bullets into separate notes.
3. Keep what is useful for future me.

## Main Question

What is useful here for future me?

## Three Questions

1. What is the point?
2. Why do I care?
3. What will I do with it?
