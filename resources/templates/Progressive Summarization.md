# Progressive Summarization

## Layer 1: Raw Capture

Paste or write the original notes here.

## Layer 2: Bold Important Points

Bold the ideas that seem useful, surprising, actionable, or reusable.

## Layer 3: Highlight the Best Parts

Use highlights for the strongest points you would want to find quickly later.

## Layer 4: Executive Summary

Write a short summary in your own words.

## Layer 5: Remix Into a Deliverable

Turn the material into an output:

- Decision
- Checklist
- Draft
- Design
- Memo
- Talk
- Plan
- Intermediate Packet

## When to Stop Summarizing

Stop when the note is useful for the next likely use case.

You do not need to process every note through every layer. Summarize more deeply only when the material is connected to an active project, an important area, or a recurring question.

Do not over-process notes before they are useful.
