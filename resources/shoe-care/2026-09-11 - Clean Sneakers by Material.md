# Clean Sneakers by Material

Protocol: [[areas/personal-development/clean-your-shoes|Clean Your Shoes]]

## Tools Needed

- Soft-bristle brush (e.g. Jason Markk Delicates brush)
- Suede brush (for suede parts)
- Suede eraser
- Clean microfiber cloth or old rag
- Old calendar pages (for stuffing while drying)

## General Steps

- Remove laces and insoles
- Brush off loose dirt/dust first (dry)
- Scrub outsole with soapy water + old toothbrush
- Spot test cleaner on a hidden area first
- Clean upper based on material:
    - Canvas/mesh/leather → soap + water, soft brush
    - Suede/nubuck → dry brush + suede eraser only, no water
- Wipe off soap residue with a damp cloth
- Hand-wash laces separately; wipe insoles (don't soak)
- Air dry stuffed with paper, away from sun/heat
- Reapply protector spray once fully dry (skip if not using one)

## Notes Per Shoe

- **New Balance 740** — soft brush + soap on mesh parts only; suede brush + eraser on suede overlay panels (side panels, toe cap area) — no water on those spots
- **Air Force 1** — full wet-clean is fine, leather handles it well; watch for the white midsole yellowing from sun exposure
- **Adidas Adizero EVO SL** — skip scrubbing entirely, just a gentle damp wipe on the thin upper; don't soak, the foam is delicate and easily damaged
- **Saucony ProGrid** — same as 740's mesh parts (soft brush + soap); only use suede tools if it has actual suede/nubuck panels
