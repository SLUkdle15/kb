All four are in pots.

## Numbers

| Plant | Light | pH | Feed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chanh vàng Mỹ — lemon (*Citrus limon*) | full sun, 6–8 h | 6.0–7.0 | citrus 2-1-1 (e.g. 6-3-3), every 4–6 weeks Mar–Sep, ~1 tbsp per 4 L of pot |
| Cây sung Mỹ — common fig (*Ficus carica*) | 8 h+ | 6.0–8.0 | 8-8-8 once in spring — skip entirely if it grew >30 cm last year |
| Dạ quỳnh — queen of the night (*Epiphyllum oxypetalum*) | bright **indirect**; sun scorches | 5.5–6.5 | max 10% N — half-strength 5-5-5 monthly, warm months only |
| Hoa hồng — rose (*Rosa* sp.) | 6 h+, **morning** sun | 6.0–7.0 | every 4–6 weeks, or after each bloom flush |

Water the same way for all: soak until it runs from the holes, then wait until the top 2–3 cm is dry. Rose at the base, early morning only. Dạ quỳnh wants cactus or orchid mix, not soil.

## The Rule in Hanoi

- **Jun–Sep** — 250+ mm rain a month. Holes clear, pots raised, no saucer, under cover in long rain.
- **Nov–Feb** — dry (Feb ~13 mm). This is when watering is actually a job.
- **Feb–Apr** — nồm, ~89% humidity. Nothing dries; rot season. Airflow, not less water.
- Pots drown in storms and dry out between them, same week. Check, don't schedule.

## Watch For

- **Lemon** — standing water kills it fast. Yellow *between* the leaf veins is micronutrient hunger (Zn, Fe, Mn), not thirst.
- **Fig** — a Mediterranean plant in a monsoon; the summer crop splits in rain, the shoulder crop is better. Excess nitrogen gives leaves and no fruit. Not the native sung (*Ficus racemosa*).
- **Dạ quỳnh** — high nitrogen or a wet winter means no flower. The cool dry Nov–Feb rest is what sets the buds, so keep it sheltered and nearly dry then.
- **Rose** — hardest of the four here. Black spot wants 24–29 °C and humidity, i.e. Hanoi Apr–Oct. Airflow and morning sun beat any spray; never wet the leaves or prune in rain.

## Prune

- **Lemon** — light, late winter, keep the centre open.
- **Fig, dạ quỳnh** — cool dry months.
- **Rose** — 60–70 days before Tết. Tết 2027 is 6 Feb, so late Nov to early Dec 2026.

## To Check

Pot size and material (feed dose is per litre; dark pots cook roots in afternoon sun), sun hours per spot, drainage, and whether any pot holds garden soil instead of potting mix — it packs solid and rots roots through nồm.
