# Habits Compound and Systems Beat Goals

Source: [[inbox/2026-07-22 - Atomic Habit|Atomic Habit]]

## Core Idea

Habits are the compound interest of self-improvement: 1% better or worse each day compounds to roughly 37 times better or worse over a year.

## Useful Details

- It doesn't matter how successful or unsuccessful you are right now. What matters is whether your habits are putting you on the path toward success — forget goals, focus on systems.
- Habits often appear to make no difference until you cross a critical threshold (the Plateau of Latent Potential) and unlock a new level of performance. This is why habits that last are hard to build: the payoff lags the work.
