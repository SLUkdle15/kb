# Four Laws of Behavior Change

Source: [[inbox/2026-07-22 - Atomic Habit|Atomic Habit]]

## Core Idea

A habit is a behavior that has been repeated enough times to become automatic. The process of habit formation begins with trial and error, and can be divided into four simple steps: cue, craving, response, and reward.

## Useful Details

Each step maps to a law of behavior change:

1. The 1st law (Cue): Make it obvious. — tactics: [[resources/behavior-change/2026-07-29 - Design Cues to Start or Break Habits|Design Cues to Start or Break Habits]]
2. The 2nd law (Craving): Make it attractive. — tactics: [[resources/behavior-change/2026-07-30 - Make Habits Attractive|Make Habits Attractive]]
3. The 3rd law (Response): Make it easy.
4. The 4th law (Reward): Make it satisfying.
