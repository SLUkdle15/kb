# Identity Change Drives Lasting Habits

Source: [[inbox/2026-07-22 - Atomic Habit|Atomic Habit]]

## Core Idea

True behavior change is identity change. If the new habit conflicts with your identity, it won't last. The very first step of building new habits is changing yourself.

## Useful Details

- Habits and identity form a two-way feedback loop: your habits shape your identity, and your identity shapes your habits. And this is done continuously.
- The two-step process to changing your identity:
  1. Decide the type of person you want to be.
  2. Prove it to yourself with small wins.
- You can work backward from your defined goals to find the type of person you become.
