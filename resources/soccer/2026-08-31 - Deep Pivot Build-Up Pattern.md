## The Pattern

The keeper plays into the deep pivot dropping short of the center circle. From there the pivot has three options, in the same priority order as [[areas/soccer/half-turn-under-pressure|Half-Turn Under Pressure]]:

1. **Forward, first choice** — split to the advanced striker in behind.
2. **Diagonal, second choice** — into the wide/half-space player higher up.
3. **Reset, last choice** — lay it back to the keeper if neither forward option is on.

The pivot's job is to receive on the half-turn so all three options are visible before the ball arrives, not to decide reactively after taking a square touch.

## Use Next

Watch for this shape specifically when playing the deep pivot role: check shoulders early, and treat the keeper as the safety valve rather than the first read.
