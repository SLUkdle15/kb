# Solo 30-Minute Session

Related area: [[areas/soccer/soccer|Soccer]]

A pre-match sharpener for a Tuesday, built to rehearse the items still open in [[resources/soccer/2026-08-04 - Soccer Playing Cues|Soccer Playing Cues]] so they can be tested in that evening's match. Not a protocol yet — run it a few times first.

Kit: ball, a wall, 4 markers, one bright object.

## 1. Activation and ball mastery — 5 min

Jog 2 min. Then in one spot, 30s each, both feet: sole rolls, inside-inside taps, toe-taps, sole-roll-to-outside-push. Finish with 10 toe-pokes at the wall from a standing ball, to make the motion familiar.

## 2. Scanning under a real stimulus — 5 min

Stand 8 yards off the wall. Bright object behind the left shoulder, marker behind the right. Before every wall pass, check one shoulder and say aloud what is there, randomizing the side. 20 reps.

A shoulder check with nothing to see trains the head turn but not the look. Give it something to report.

## 3. Receiving-foot A/B test — 8 min

Marker 2 yards in front as the presser. Pass into the wall, receive with the first touch turning away from the marker, second touch into a 3-yard target gate to the side.

- Block A: 12 reps receiving on the **back foot**.
- Block B: 12 reps receiving on the **main foot**.

Score each block by how many turns were clean on one touch. This is the drill that matters — it turns the two untested candidates in [[resources/soccer/2026-08-04 - Soccer Playing Cues|Soccer Playing Cues]] into a number.

## 4. Half-turn to three options, in order — 6 min

Add markers at forward, diagonal, and back. Wall pass, half-turn first touch, then play the first available gate in the priority order from [[areas/soccer/half-turn-under-pressure|Half-Turn Under Pressure]]: forward, then diagonal, then reset. Name the choice out loud before the second touch. 15 reps.

End every rep with the 3-step rule — three steps immediately after the pass. The habit only survives a match if it is never repped without the steps.

## 5. Pre-fake — 6 min

Ball at feet, marker as defender. Two runs, not one: two steps the wrong way, then accelerate past the marker and strike. 10 each side.

The smallest concrete version of the bare "add a pre-fake" directive: the fake is the two steps you do not want.

## Record After the Match

- Which receiving foot won Block A vs Block B.
- Whether that held up against a real presser.
