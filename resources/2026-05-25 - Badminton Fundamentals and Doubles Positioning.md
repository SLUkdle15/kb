
# Badminton Fundamentals and Doubles Positioning

Related area: [[badminton]]

### Guide

- Learn the 7 basic shot types: clears, drops, smashes, drives, blocks, nets, and lifts.
- Integrate shots with footwork from the middle.
- Know how to get to any corner from the starting position, then follow your shot.
- Practice drills.
- Build strategy.

### Swing

- Set up with a slight wrist flex and a slightly angled racket head.
- Hold the racket mainly with two fingers: thumb and index. Keep the other fingers relaxed.
- Open up your left hand.
- Wait, find the timing, and match the shuttlecock’s speed.
- Short exhale on contact. Lightly brace your core when turning your torso.

### Footwork

- Use wide split steps.
- Push off powerfully.
- Stay low during shuffles.
- Rebound powerfully.
    - On the bound-back recovery, keep the non-racket foot light with the toe pointing slightly down, ready to land and push off again.
- Slow your momentum to reset.

### Doubles

- Always prepare after serving.
- In defense or attack mode, look for the hardest place to put the shuttlecock.
- On defense, cover the straight-line attack and leave the cross-court shot for your partner.
- If you make a bad lift, prepare for a smash.

- Front player:
    - When your rear partner is about to smash, move from "net hunting" to "intercepting position."
    - Cover the straight side first; cross-court replies travel longer, so you have more time.
    - Expect a push, drive, or kill.
