# Inbox

## What Belongs Here

Use the Inbox for fast capture before you know where something belongs.

Examples:

- Quick ideas
- Raw meeting notes
- Web highlights to process later
- Questions
- Draft thoughts
- Screenshots or attachment references that need review

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Notes already tied to an active project
- Stable responsibilities that belong in Areas
- Reference material ready to file in Resources
- Completed or inactive material

## Review Frequency

Review at least weekly using [[Resources/Templates/Weekly Review]].

If the Inbox is growing quickly, clear it daily.
